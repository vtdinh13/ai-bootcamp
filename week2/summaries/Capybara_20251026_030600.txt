Source: Capybara

The capybara (_Hydrochoerus hydrochaeris_) is the largest rodent native to South America, often found near water and living in social groups. It is semi-aquatic and has a distinctive barrel-shaped body, reddish-brown fur above and yellowish-brown below. Capybaras are herbivorous, feeding mainly on grasses and aquatic plants and are known for their docile nature. Though not endangered, they are hunted for meat and skin. The capybara also features prominently in popular culture and internet memes.
